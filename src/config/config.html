KawKaw

How it works

Viewers type !call and !shoo in chat to push KawKaw toward licking the streamer or fleeing. Game tuning (trigger, meter speed, session length) lives in the backend's .env for now. This page sets where KawKaw appears on the overlay.

Overlay position

Positions are 0–1 fractions of the overlay (0 = left/top, 1 = right/bottom).
